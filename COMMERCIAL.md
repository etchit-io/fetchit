## When you probably want the commercial license

- You want to ship fetch>it (or `fetchit-core` / `fetchit-net` /
  `fetchit-ffi`) as part of a closed-source application or service.
- You want to provide a hosted service built on fetch>it without
  publishing your service's source code to its users.
- Your legal/procurement process forbids AGPL-licensed code in
  production.
- You want commercial support, indemnification, or warranty terms that
  the AGPL explicitly does not provide.

If none of those apply -- use the AGPL track. No fee, no signature, no
permission needed.

## How to get a commercial license

Email **licensing@etchit.io** with:

- the product or service you intend to embed fetch>it in;
- approximate deployment scale (per-seat / per-instance / total
  user count, whichever you measure);
- which fetch>it components you'll use (`fetchit-core`, `fetchit-ffi`,
  the desktop app, the Android app, the browser extension, or some
  combination).

You'll get a quote, terms, and a signed license agreement. Pricing is
case-by-case while the project is in beta; we don't publish a
standard rate card yet.
